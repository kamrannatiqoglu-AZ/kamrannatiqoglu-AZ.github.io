Pomegranate Garden — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Pomegranate Garden ("the game") does not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email or location. Game progress and settings are saved only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address and usage data to show and measure ads. In regions where it is required (e.g. the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the game deletes all data stored by the game on your device. You can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.
