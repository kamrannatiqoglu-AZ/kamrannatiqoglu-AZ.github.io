1000 Block — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1000 Block ("the game") is a block puzzle game developed by Kamran Khalilov. The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. Your levels, scores, coins, boosters and settings are saved only on your device. The game works offline.

Google Play Games (optional)

On Android the game can use Google Play Games to show the world leaderboard. If you use it, Google provides your Play Games display name and your score to the leaderboard under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We do not run our own servers and do not receive your email address or password. The game works fully without it.

Advertising and purchases

The current version shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing. If this changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

All game data is stored on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.
