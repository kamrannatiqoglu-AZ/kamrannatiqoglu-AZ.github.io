Bilik Arenası — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Bilik Arenası ("the game") is a trivia quiz game developed by Kamran Khalilov. The game does not ask you for your email, phone number or location. Your scores, coins, records and settings are saved only on your device. The questions work offline.

Google Play Games sign-in (optional)

You can sign in with Google Play Games. When you sign in, the game receives your Play Games display name and profile picture from Google and shows them in the game menu. They are kept only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive your email address or password. Sign-in is optional — the game works fully without it.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob (rewarded ads you choose to watch, and short ads between questions). AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address and usage data to show and measure ads. In regions where it is required (e.g. the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings → Reset progress to delete your scores, coins and records from your device. Uninstalling the game deletes all data stored on your device. You can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.
