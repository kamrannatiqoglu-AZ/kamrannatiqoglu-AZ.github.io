Pomegranate Garden — Delete your account and data

Developer: Kamran Khalilov

Delete in the game

  1. Open Pomegranate Garden.
  2. Tap Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the game, send an email to kamrannatiqoglu@gmail.com with the subject "Pomegranate Garden – delete my data" and your Google Play Games display name. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your game progress: levels, stars, points, items, daily rewards and quests.
  • Your cloud save stored in Google Play Games.

Nothing is kept after deletion. Your Google account itself is managed by Google and is not affected. Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google; you can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
